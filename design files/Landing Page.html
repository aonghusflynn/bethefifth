Common questions.

Is it really free?

Yes — for players it's free forever. For organisers, posting and filling games will always be free. We only charge organisers who want to collect pitch fees through the app or run recurring private games.

Where are you available?

We're launching in Dublin in 2026. Manchester and London are next, followed by Amsterdam and Berlin. Sign up to hear when we land in your city.

Is it only 5-a-side?

Five-a-side is our home. We'll support 7-a-side and 11-a-side as the community grows — the platform handles any pitch size.

What if someone no-shows?

Every player has a public reliability score. Cancel late or no-show repeatedly and your profile reflects it — organisers can filter on it. The best players rise to the top.

How do payments work?

In Phase 1 there are no in-app payments — players just pay the organiser the way they always have. Pro organisers will be able to collect pitch fees via Stripe at booking time (coming soon).
