BeTheFifth.

Brand exploration For Aonghus — Dublin
Round 01 · Apr 2026

A brand for the game that almost didn't happen. BeTheFifth is built around one small, specific feeling: we're a player short — and the tiny hero who fixes it. Three directions below, sharing a spine: confident typography, one hot accent, zero noise.

Story & principles

01 / 06

We're one short. Pitch booked, mates warming up, WhatsApp going nowhere. The fifth player is the difference between a match and a write-off. BeTheFifth is the platform for the person who shows up — and the organiser who stops begging.

— Origin note
01 · Principle

Organiser first.

Every decision answers one question: does this make it easier to fill the squad tonight?

02 · Principle

Play, not product.

We talk like mates in a group chat, not a SaaS changelog. Short sentences. Earned energy.

03 · Principle

The fifth is the hero.

The brand celebrates the late yes, the reliable player, the one who saves the match. Not the scoreline.

04 · Principle

Local feel, global frame.

Feels at home in Dublin, Manchester, Berlin. No shamrocks. The community is the color.

Voice & taglines

05 / 06

Three voice modes for three moments — the hook, the nudge, the welcome. All of them sound like someone you'd actually play with.

Mode A · Confident hook
Find a game.
Play tonight.
Two beats. For landing pages, app store, hero. Works across EU markets.
Mode B · Cheeky nudge
Four lads,
one pitch, one missing.
For social, push notifications, referral. The organiser's voice.
Mode C · Welcome
Show up.
Be the fifth.
For onboarding, thank-you states, post-match. The player's voice.
Do
  • Short sentences. Earned exclamations.
  • Name the feeling (pitch booked, one short).
  • Celebrate the late yes.
Don't
  • Call users "athletes" or "warriors".
  • Say "platform", "ecosystem", "community-driven".
  • Use emoji. The type does the work.
